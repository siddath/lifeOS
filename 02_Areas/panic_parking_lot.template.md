# Panic Parking Lot

> An optional place to write a worry and decide what, if anything, would help. You can pause, seek support, take a manageable action or leave it for later. No productivity target is required.

**How to use:** add a row when you want to. Optional labels: `regret` · `fear` · `comparison` · `task` · `unsure`. Choose your own words and response; recording the thought is enough.

## Fictional examples (adapt or remove)

| Panic thought | Type | Action |
|---------------|------|--------|
| I've wasted so much time | regret | Pause. I do not owe work as repayment. Decide whether rest, support or a small next step would help. |
| What if the launch flops | fear | List 3 things I can actually control this week; do the first. |
| I need it all figured out *now* | unsure | Write the question down. Choose whether it needs attention today. |
| Everyone my age is ahead of me | comparison | Compare to last-year me, not to a highlight reel. |

## Optional reframes

**Why one event feels like everything.** The mind attaches too many symbolic questions to a single moment — *Will I make it? Did I waste years? Will people see me as a success?* That's too much load for one interview, launch, or decision. **One gate, not the kingdom.**

**Stop making every event a referendum on your worth.** A launch is not "am I good enough?" — it's only "can I ship this one thing well this week?"

**Regret does not create a work debt.** Notice what you need now. A pause, a conversation or a small action can each be a reasonable choice.
